# One-minute Samewise demo

**Target:** 56 seconds of product-only screen recording at a consistent desktop viewport. Use deterministic visible synthetic CSVs. Record cursor actions without browser chrome, private tabs, personal data, hidden truth files, or API keys. Narration can be recorded separately.

| Time | Screen and action | Narration / optional caption | Cut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | Upload the two visible organization fixture CSVs; click Upload & profile. | “Two files describe overlapping organizations, with different columns and messy values.” | Cut after profiles appear. |
| 6–14 s | Match setup: pan across confirmed correspondences, **Use to match**, **Keep in result**, and semantic family. | “Samewise learns which fields can establish identity and which values belong in the result.” | Cut before loading spinner. |
| 14–21 s | Run matching; show compact result summary and automatic versus review outcomes. | “Indexed candidates and field-level evidence handle clear matches, while weak cases are held back.” | Cut directly into review. |
| 21–33 s | Open one uncertain case. Hold on side-by-side A/B values, agreement and contradiction, then point to Same/Different. | “A reviewer sees why a pair is uncertain before deciding whether it is the same entity.” | Do not click a decision solely for the shot. |
| 33–42 s | Show a review group; preview bounded examples and eligibility, then confirm an eligible batch only in the prepared demo run. | “Repeated evidence patterns can be handled together, with a human decision recorded for every eligible pair.” | Cut after progress updates. |
| 42–50 s | Merge values: select a field rule, show preview counts, explicitly apply. | “Identity is settled first. Value conflicts get a separate, previewed merge plan.” | Cut to Export. |
| 50–56 s | Show Ready to export and the reconciled CSV, report, and manifest actions. | “The final data is gated; the report and hashed manifest preserve what happened.” | End on product screen. |

Rehearse with a deterministic synthetic fixture that actually has an eligible review group, such as the documented public-style workload; verify its visible source inputs and group eligibility in the UI.
